Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

DJ AI Partnership LLC ("DJAI", "we") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect when you visit jdaipartnership.com, apply as a contributor or contact us, and how we use it.

1. Who we are

DJ AI Partnership LLC, a New Mexico limited liability company, 1209 Mountain Road Pl NE, Ste N, Albuquerque, NM 87110, USA. Contact: contact@jdaipartnership.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Account and application data: name, email, phone, country, city, languages, devices and referral code.
  • Project data: videos, audio and related metadata you record for a project, only after you sign that project's consent and rights agreement.
  • Payment data needed to pay you (for example your payout account email). We never store full card numbers.
  • Basic technical data such as browser type and pages visited.

3. How we use it

To review applications, assign and coordinate projects, review quality, pay contributors, answer questions, keep the service secure and meet legal obligations.

4. Recordings and consent

No recording is made without the contributor's signed consent. Contributors must not film people, faces, documents or places that have not agreed to be recorded, and must follow each project's exclusions. Recordings are delivered only to the client of the project they were made for, under a written agreement.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with service providers that help us run the platform (hosting, email, payments), with project clients as described above, or when the law requires it.

6. Retention and security

We keep data only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, and protect it with access controls and encrypted connections.

7. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or close your account, by writing to contact@jdaipartnership.com. We reply within 30 days.

8. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
