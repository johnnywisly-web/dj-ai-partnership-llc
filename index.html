About

What is DJ AI Partnership LLC?

DJ AI Partnership LLC is a limited liability company working at the intersection of artificial intelligence and real-world work. It recruits independent contractors for AI data collection projects, and partners with local businesses — JL Fitness, Loochen Beauty and Ferme Agronomie de L'UNIFA — to give AI better, real-world data. Motto: Powering the Future Together.

01 · The Opportunity

Contribute to AI through real-world work.

We are looking for individuals who can access a genuine work environment and record approved real-world manual activities for AI data collection projects. This opportunity is designed for independent contractors and individual participants — you do not need to represent a company to apply.
