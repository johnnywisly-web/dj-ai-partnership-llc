A paid independent contractor opportunity. You don't need to represent a company to apply, just access to a genuine work environment and the right equipment.

Create your account

Sign up with your personal email and complete your profile: contact details, location, smartphone, headstrap, weekly availability and payout method.

Get reviewed & onboarded

Our team reviews your eligibility and contacts you by email with next steps, project instructions and the agreements to sign.

Record & get paid

Responsible data

Responsible by design.

Good AI data starts with respect for the people and places in it. Every project follows the same ground rules.

Only record activities and locations you are authorized to record. Never submit confidential client information, passwords, credentials, private personal information, trade secrets or protected material.

Agreements before recording

Consent and data-assignment agreements are signed before any recording begins.

Authorized recording

Contributors must have permission to record the environment and activities in a project.

Protect sensitive information

No intentional capture of confidential, private or restricted information.

Follow project instructions

Every contractor follows the safety, privacy and data-handling requirements of their project.
