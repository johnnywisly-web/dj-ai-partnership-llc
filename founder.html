About Johnny

Johnny Wisly Derival is a Haitian entrepreneur based in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic. He is the founder and CEO of DJ AI Partnership LLC, a New Mexico company that collects egocentric (POV) video data in real homes and workplaces to help train the next generation of AI and robots.

Before founding DJ AI Partnership, Johnny spent more than five years as a Haitian Creole and French interpreter for medical, legal and educational settings, and worked in customer service and sales for a major U.S. telecom provider. He holds a degree in agronomy and has trained AI models as a data contributor himself, which is how he saw the need for a trusted, well-managed contributor network.

What he is building

Johnny's vision is simple: AI should learn from real people doing real work, and the people who make that data should be treated and paid fairly. Under his leadership DJ AI Partnership recruits, trains and coordinates contributors, reviews every recording for quality, and secures signed consent before any data is collected. The company also offers translation, transcription and language data in Haitian Creole, French, English and Spanish.

DJ AI Partnership keeps growing every week, with new contributors joining from the Caribbean, the Americas, Africa, Europe and Asia.
